Netherlands NL

Scouting Nederland

The national scout organisation, which includes water scout groups (waterscouting).

Potential benefit: local contacts, volunteers, organising water activities, support for beginners.

Language: DutchOfficial website

SailWise

An organisation specialising in accessible water activities for people with disabilities.

Potential benefit: experience of adaptive sailing, specialised equipment, advice on accessibility.

Language: DutchOfficial website

Jeugdfonds Sport & Cultuur

A fund that helps reduce financial barriers to the participation of children and young people in sports and cultural activities.

Potential benefit: checking the possibility of supporting participants from low-income families. Age, territorial and other criteria need to be clarified.

Official website

Jeugdfonds Sport & Cultuur Amsterdam

A regional branch of the fund that supports children from Amsterdam in accessing sport and cultural activities.

Potential benefit: an example of a regional system for funding children's sports activities, which can be studied in order to adapt a similar model in other communities.

Official website

Sport- en cultuurtegoed, gemeente Rotterdam

Financial support for children from low-income families in Rotterdam to do sport.

Potential benefit: an example of a municipal mechanism for funding children's leisure, which can be useful for developing programmes that make sport accessible to children.

Official website

Sailability Netherlands

An initiative that promotes the accessibility of sailing for people with disabilities. The website collects information about places where you can do adapted sailing.

Potential benefit: finding accessible locations, learning about inclusive sailing practices and the chance to build cooperation with clubs that work with people with disabilities.

Official website

Kidsproof: Watersporten Eindhoven

An information resource listing children's clubs and sports activities in the Eindhoven region, including water sports.

Potential benefit: finding children's sports clubs, getting to know the available water activities and possible partners for organising sessions.

Official website

Watersportverbond: Optimist on Tour

An initiative of the Dutch Water Sports Association aimed at popularising sailing among children and young people.

Potential benefit: teaching materials, experience of popularising sailing, organising introductory sessions and attracting children to water sports.

Official website

Optimist on Tour

An educational and sports programme introducing children of cub age to sailing.

Potential benefit: an example of how to organise first sailing sessions for children of cub age, a chance to borrow the format of short practical sessions and sports events.

Official website

Watersportvereniging Rotterdam (Kralingse Plas)

A water sports club in Rotterdam connected with the development of water sports on Lake Kralingse Plas.

Potential benefit: a potential partner for learning about the organisation of water sports activities, club infrastructure and ways to involve children in water sports.

Official website

Scouting President F. D. Roosevelt

A scout group in the Netherlands focused on children and young people with physical disabilities.

Potential benefit: an example of inclusive scouting, adapting scout sessions and creating opportunities for children with disabilities to take part fully in the youth movement.

Official website

NJi: Nederlands Jeugdinstituut (Diversiteit)

A Dutch youth institute that carries out research, develops recommendations and spreads knowledge about the development and wellbeing of children and young people. The Diversiteit section is devoted to diversity and inclusion.

Potential benefit: teaching materials, research and recommendations on working with different groups of children, creating an inclusive environment and ensuring equal opportunities.

Official website

Scouting, Diversity & Inclusion (WOSM)

Methodological materials of the World Organization of the Scout Movement (WOSM) on diversity, equality and inclusion in scouting.

Potential benefit: recommendations on adapting scout programmes, involving children with different needs, creating a safe environment and ensuring equal participation of all children.

Official website

Scouting Nederland: Champions for Nature

An educational pack from Dutch scouting devoted to environmental education and fostering a responsible attitude to nature, based on the UN Sustainable Development Goals.

Potential benefit: ready-made ideas for scout sessions, environmental challenges, practical tasks for children and the chance to integrate environmental education into the leadership programme.

Official website

Sailability: an example of adapted boats (WSV Hoorn)

A sailing club that develops adaptive sailing and uses specially equipped boats for people with disabilities.

Potential benefit: a practical example of adapting yachts and sports equipment, getting to know technical solutions for making sailing accessible and the chance to borrow experience for inclusive water sessions.

Official website

Spain ES

Scouts de España (ASDE)

A scout federation with regional organisations.

Potential benefit: local volunteers, inclusive youth methods, help with communicating with communities.

Official website

Cruz Roja Española

An organisation that runs social and humanitarian programmes, including for people in vulnerable situations.

Potential benefit: advice on supporting displaced young people, language and social barriers, and available local services.

Official website

Fundación ONCE

An organisation whose work is connected with inclusion and accessibility for people with disabilities.

For the Valencia region it is also worth contacting local youth centres, municipalities and sailing clubs to check the possibility of discounted sessions and the accessibility of specific beaches or marinas.

Potential benefit: methodological resources, advice on physical accessibility and on organising activities inclusively.

Official website

IBSA Iberia – Inclusive Sailing Academy

An initiative created in cooperation with Escola de Vela Adaptada de Sitges (Barcelona), aimed at developing adaptive sailing among children and young people with disabilities. The programme combines regular sailing sessions, summer camps and educational activities.

Potential benefit: one of the most interesting examples to follow. A chance to study a ready-made model of an inclusive sailing academy, how sessions are organised, how children's independence is developed and how sport is combined with social integration.

Official website

Escola de Vela Adaptada de Sitges

An adaptive sailing school in Sitges (Catalonia) that works with children and young people with disabilities. In cooperation with IBSA Iberia it runs regular training, summer camps and educational sessions. Among other equipment it uses specialised Hansa 303 boats.

Potential benefit: practical experience of organising inclusive sessions, adapting water equipment, training instructors and ensuring participants' independence on the water. A chance to arrange an introductory visit or an exchange of experience.

Official website

YES WE SAIL

An initiative from Barcelona that develops inclusive sailing, especially for people with visual impairments. It works on adapting sailing with special technologies and tactile feedback systems.

Potential benefit: getting to know technological solutions for adapting sailing, creating an accessible environment for people with visual impairments and developing inclusive sports programmes.

Official website

Escola de Vela i Caiac Sant Pol – Costa Brava

A water sports school on the Costa Brava coast offering children's sessions in sailing, kayaking, SUP and other water activities. It organises children's sports programmes and themed camps.

Potential benefit: ready-made formats of children's water camps, a chance to organise introductory sailing sessions and to combine several water disciplines in one programme. Inclusiveness needs to be checked separately.

Official website

Slovenský skauting

Potential benefit: local scout groups, volunteers, youth educational practices and organisational support.

IUVENTA – Slovenský inštitút mládeže

Karpatská nadácia

A foundation that supports the development of communities, civil society and local initiatives.

Potential benefit: financial support for projects, grant opportunities, advice and help in carrying out community initiatives.

Official website

Fond na podporu kultúry národnostných menšín (KULT MINOR)

A Slovak state fund that supports the culture of national minorities.

Potential benefit: grant funding for cultural, educational and artistic projects, including initiatives of the Ukrainian national minority.

Official website

NYS – Škola jachtingu

A sailing school offering training and practical sailing sessions.

Potential benefit: teaching children and young people the basics of sailing, organising practical sessions and involving them in active water-based leisure.

Official website

Yacht Club Sĺňava Piešťany

A yacht club that develops sailing and organises activities on the water.

Potential benefit: sports infrastructure, coaches, running sessions and camps for children and young people, opportunities for practical sailing training.

Official website

Yacht Academy

A Slovak sailing school that organises courses, children's camps and sailing training.

Potential benefit: professional instructors, training programmes, organising sports camps and involving young people in water sports.

Official website

Veľká Domaša

A reservoir in the Prešov region, important for recreation, tourism and water sports.

Potential benefit: space for water activities, sports events and youth camps. At the same time, a low water level can create obstacles for navigation, recreation and the operation of water infrastructure.

Official website

Yacht Club Košice

A potential sports organisation for developing sailing and water sports in the Košice region.

Potential benefit: involving coaches and athletes, organising sessions for children and young people, developing water leisure. The exact name of the club and its activities should be checked additionally here.

Official website

IPčko Prešov

A non-profit organisation that provides psychological and crisis help to people, including children and young people.

Potential benefit: psychological support for children and young people, anti-bullying work, work on emotional health, running educational workshops, crisis counselling and creating a safe, inclusive environment.

Official website

Poland PL

Związek Harcerstwa Polskiego (ZHP)

The Polish scout organisation.

Potential benefit: local scout units, volunteer support, organising camps and water sessions.

Official website

Fundacja Integracja

An organisation working on accessibility and support for people with disabilities.

Potential benefit: materials and advice on the accessibility of locations, transport and inclusive communication.

Official website

Polski Czerwony Krzyż

The Polish Red Cross.

Potential benefit: finding local social support programmes and information for people who have experienced forced displacement.

Official website

Związek Harcerstwa Polskiego (ZHP) – Chorągiew Gdańska

A regional structure of the national scout organisation ZHP that involves volunteers in work for children and young people. Volunteering is possible even without having to join the organisation.

Potential benefit: involving volunteers to accompany children's groups, register participants, help with organisation on the shore, team activities and communication with young people. A chance to partly overcome a shortage of volunteers and language barriers.

Official website

Regionalne Centrum Wolontariatu w Gdańsku

A regional volunteering centre in Gdańsk that coordinates volunteer initiatives, organises training and helps non-governmental organisations find volunteers.

Potential benefit: finding volunteers for logistics, translation, communication with families, accompanying participants and organising events. It can help to draw up a list of volunteer roles and to spread a recruitment announcement.

Official website

Państwowy Fundusz Rehabilitacji Osób Niepełnosprawnych (PFRON)

A Polish state fund that finances projects of non-governmental organisations aimed at the social and professional rehabilitation of people with disabilities. Support areas include sports training, cultural events and training of specialists.

Potential benefit: funding for adapted sports sessions, inclusive events, staff training and other costs provided for by the terms of a particular call. A Polish non-governmental organisation that meets the fund's requirements is needed in order to take part.

Official website

Ministerstwo Sportu i Turystyki – „Promocja Sportu Osób z Niepełnosprawnościami w 2026 r.”

A state programme of the Polish Ministry of Sport and Tourism aimed at popularising sport among people with disabilities through integration events, information campaigns, training and exchange of experience.

Potential benefit: potential funding for open inclusive sports events, information campaigns on the accessibility of sailing, training and introductory visits. The programme requires its own contribution of at least 5%.

Official website

Sopocki Klub Żeglarski ERGO Hestia (SKŻ)

A sailing club and water sports centre in Sopot with sections for windsurfing, kitesurfing, wing, Open Skiff and SUP. It works with children, young people, athletes and amateurs and organises summer sports programmes.

Potential benefit: access to sports infrastructure, water, equipment and coaches' experience. A chance to organise trial sessions in sailing and water disciplines. Especially interesting as a potential partner for the practical part of the project.

Official website

RewaSurf – water sports school

A water sports school in Rewa that offers sessions and rental of sports equipment. It is located by the bay, which creates opportunities for practical water sports sessions.

Potential benefit: access to instructors, equipment and practical training without having to buy your own gear. A chance to organise trial sessions for children and young people, including within an inclusive programme.

Official website

Ukraine UA

Plast — the National Scout Organisation of Ukraine

A youth organisation with a network of local branches.

Potential benefit: involving leaders, supporting young people, organising camps and non-formal education.

Official website

Ukrainian Red Cross

A humanitarian organisation that provides various kinds of support to people affected by crisis situations.

Potential benefit: information about local services, psychosocial support, help for displaced persons.

Official website

SpivDiia

A Ukrainian initiative connected with humanitarian, social and psycho-emotional support.

Potential benefit: checking available programmes for young people and the possibilities of referral to appropriate services.

Official website

Family Yacht Club: the "Power of Wind" programme

A yacht club in Kyiv that teaches sailing and develops adaptive sailing. The "Power of Wind" programme uses sailing as a tool for recovery, rehabilitation and building confidence. Adapted sessions on boats for people with disabilities are provided.

Potential benefit: exchange of experience in adaptive sailing, use of specially equipped boats, organising inclusive sessions and involving people with disabilities in water sports.

Official website

Odesa Sailing Center (Odesa Sailing Center NGO)

An organisation in Odesa that develops sailing, including adaptive sailing and a children's and youth strand. Its priorities include creating opportunities for people with disabilities to take part and developing young athletes.

Potential benefit: partnership in adaptive sailing, exchange of experience on rehabilitation through sport, developing children's programmes and organising inclusive water activities.

Official website

Odesa Sailing Team

A children's and youth sailing school in Odesa that teaches children and teenagers to sail Optimist and ILCA class boats, prepares athletes for regattas and runs introductory sessions.

Potential benefit: methods of teaching sailing to children, involving coaches, organising introductory sessions and exchanging experience on the sports training of young people. The first introductory training is stated to be free by prior arrangement.

Official website

Lviv Yacht School: Wind Hunters

A sailing school from Lviv, listed in the international catalogue of ISSA (International Sailing School Association).

Potential benefit: a potential partner for exchanging experience in sailing training, finding instructors and developing programmes that introduce children and young people to sailing.

Official website

Austria AT

Pfadfinder und Pfadfinderinnen Österreichs (PPÖ)

The Austrian scout organisation.

Potential benefit: local groups, volunteers, youth methods and organising joint activities.

Official website

Österreichisches Jugendrotkreuz

The Austrian Youth Red Cross.

Potential benefit: educational materials, youth support and resources related to safety and wellbeing.

Official website

Österreichischer Behindertensportverband (ÖBSV)

The Austrian sports organisation for people with disabilities.

Potential benefit: advice on adaptive sport, finding suitable clubs and instructors.

Official website

Association of Ukrainian Organisations in Austria (Verband der ukrainischen Organisationen in Österreich)

An association founded on 22 January 2025 that unites Ukrainian civic organisations from different cities in Austria. Its members include Plast in Austria. The main aim of the association is to coordinate the work of Ukrainian organisations, to represent the interests of the Ukrainian community and to organise joint events.

Potential benefit: building contacts with Ukrainian communities and civic organisations in Austria, finding partners and volunteers, spreading information about the project, and involving Ukrainian young people in international initiatives.

Official website

Austrian National Federation of Scouts and Guides (Pfadfinder und Pfadfinderinnen Österreichs — PPÖ)

The largest national scout organisation in Austria, officially represented in the World Organization of the Scout Movement (WOSM) and the World Association of Girl Guides and Girl Scouts (WAGGGS). Together with Plast in Austria it took part in the inter-organisational event "An Bam Aufstön 2.0" on 23 April 2026 in Vienna.

Potential benefit: organising joint camps, training for leaders, youth exchanges, taking part in international scout events and forums, exchanging experience in work with children and young people.

Official website

Austrian Scout Guild (Pfadfinder-Gilde Österreichs — PGÖ)

An association of adult scouts that supports the scout movement, promotes cooperation between generations and involves adults in civic activity. The guild also took part in the joint event "An Bam Aufstön 2.0" in 2026.

Potential benefit: mentoring for young leaders, support for volunteers, passing on experience, organisational help, intergenerational projects and joint charity events.

Official website
